## 2. Voice Principles

| Principle | Rule | Example (Big Four Standard) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authoritative & Lucid** | Direct sentences without marketing hype. | *"We structure tax positions that withstand regulatory scrutiny."* |
| **Strategic Governance** | Tax framed as balance-sheet risk management. | *"Aligning corporate structures with DJP and OECD standards."* |
| **Partner-Led Precision** | Direct engagement with senior advisors. | *"Every client engagement is directed by senior tax partners."* |
| **Bilingual Accuracy** | Native statutory terms with plain explanations. | *"Comprehensive defense for SP2DK inquiries and Tax Court appeals."* |

## 3. Practice Areas

### Pillar 1: Corporate Tax Advisory & Compliance
*Kepatuhan & Penasihat Pajak Perusahaan*

#### English (EN)
- **Tagline**: End-to-end corporate tax compliance and risk mitigation.
- **Summary**: Complete corporate income tax (CIT), withholding tax, and VAT governance designed to eliminate audit exposure.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Annual and Periodic Tax Returns (SPT Tahunan & Masa)
  - Pre-Audit Diagnostic Reviews & Tax Health Checks
  - Domestic Tax Planning & Withholding Tax Governance
  - Regulatory Impact Assessments (UU HPP & PMK updates)

#### Indonesian (ID)
- **Tagline**: Tata kelola kepatuhan pajak korporasi terpadu dan mitigasi risiko.
- **Summary**: Pengelolaan Pajak Penghasilan (PPh Badan), pemotongan/pemungutan, dan PPN secara menyeluruh guna mencegah risiko pemeriksaan.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Pelaporan SPT Tahunan PPh Badan & SPT Masa
  - *Tax Health Check* & Diagnostik Kepatuhan
  - Perencanaan Pajak Domestik & Tata Kelola *Withholding Tax*
  - Analisis Dampak Regulasi Perpajakan (UU HPP & PMK)
